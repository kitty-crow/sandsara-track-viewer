A complete local workflow

Move from a photograph or drawing to a native Sandsara binary track, then inspect the exact route before placing it on the table.

01

Find the essential lines

Upload PNG, JPEG, BMP, WebP or GIF artwork. Refine contrast, thresholding, contours and simplification, then download clean SVG line art.

 02

Compose a continuous path

Sample SVG geometry, fit it inside the circular drawing area, join separated shapes and download a native Sandsara .bin.

 03

Observe every movement

Decode and validate an existing track locally. Review the route, point count, coordinate range, start, finish and drawing-radius warnings.
